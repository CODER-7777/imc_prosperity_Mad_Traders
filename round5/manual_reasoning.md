# Manual Challenge Allocation Reasoning

We are allocating **11% to every single good**, resulting in a **99% total budget utilization (990,000 XIRECs)**.

### 1. Why 11% Each?
The manual challenge on Ignith charges a **quadratic trading fee** based on the volume percentage:
`Fee = (percentage / 100)^2 * 1,000,000`

Because the fee is squared, putting a large percentage into one product is financial suicide. 
- If you put 100% into one good: Fee = 1,000,000 (Wipes out your entire budget instantly).
- If you put 50% into one good: Fee = 250,000.
- If you put 11% into one good: Fee = 12,100.

By spreading the budget across 9 goods at 11% each, we minimize the quadratic penalty. Total fees are just `12,100 * 9 = 108,900`. We invest 990,000 XIRECs with only 108,900 in fees, leaving plenty of room for massive net profits.
